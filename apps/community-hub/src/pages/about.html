01 Principles

What Polaris will and won’t do

  1. It loads the real game.

    Polaris opens the official Kirka.io website. It doesn’t host, copy or modify the game.

  2. Cosmetic only.

    Crosshairs, HUD layout, skins and themes change how things look on your screen — never hitboxes, movement or anything the server controls.

  3. No cheats, no automation.

    No aim assistance, no macros, no anti-cheat bypasses. Plugins that try are removed.

  4. Signed updates.

    Every release and configuration bundle is signed; the client refuses anything that doesn’t verify.
