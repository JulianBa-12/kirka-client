What the Discord is for

# releases
Previews of upcoming builds and beta testing before a version reaches the stable channel.
# support
Quick questions and help from other players. Reproducible bugs still go to the bug tracker.
# skins
Work-in-progress skins and crosshairs before they’re uploaded to the gallery.

Server channel names may differ. Same rules as everywhere on Polaris: no cheats, no automation, no anti-cheat discussion.
